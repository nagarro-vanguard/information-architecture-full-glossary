# Glossary - Residential (ANACITY Community Management, DIEZ Dubai)

**Last updated:** 2026-09-29

This glossary defines the vocabulary of the ANACITY Community Management platform as deployed for DIEZ, Dubai. It serves business analysts, product and delivery teams working the Residential deployment, and anyone reading its requirements: a reader can look up any thing the platform stores or acts on (object), any kind of person it serves (role), any stage of the work (step), and any named condition or value-set (state). It is written in Residential's own words (community, block, resident, Community Administrator, Gatekeeper App, notice, Complaint Box, welcome email, Mollak, Multi Property Dashboard); where a concept is shared with the Commercial deployment, the entry carries an `Other deployment:` pointer to the Commercial word. This is a Gate 2 draft, pending review before it graduates to the output glossary.
