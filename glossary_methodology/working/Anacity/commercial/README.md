# Glossary - Commercial (ANACITY Business, Embassy SYNC / Embassy REIT)

**Last updated:** 2026-09-29

This glossary defines, in the Commercial deployment's own words, every term the ANACITY Business platform ("Embassy SYNC", operated for Embassy REIT) uses - the things it stores and acts on, the kinds of person it serves, the stages of work, and the named conditions and value-sets. It serves business analysts, product and delivery teams, and reviewers who need one settled meaning per word. A reader can look up any object, role-group, step, or state, see how it connects to the rest, and - for a concept shared with the Residential deployment - follow the `Other deployment:` pointer to the Residential word (the two dialects are kept separate, and neither is retired). This is the Gate 2 draft, produced from the nine evidence-backed section drafts; it is derived from and must not contradict the source below.
